Privacy Policy
Support Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Par Card is an app made by For Real Life Studio ("we"). This policy explains what information Par Card uses and how. In short: we don't collect any of your data. It stays on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you use iCloud sync.

Information stored on your device

Par Card saves the player names you enter, your scores and rounds, courses you add, and your favorite courses. This information is stored on your device. It is never sent to us, and we can't see it.

iCloud sync (Par Card Pro)

If you have Par Card Pro and are signed in to iCloud, your saved rounds, players, added courses and favorites are also stored in your own iCloud account so they appear on your other devices and come back on a new device. This data is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We have no access to it. The round you're currently scoring is not synced. You can turn off iCloud for Par Card at any time in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Location

If you allow it, Par Card uses your location while the app is open to show the mini golf courses closest to you and to pin courses you add. Your location is processed only on your device. It is never sent to us or to third parties, and it is not used for tracking or advertising. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings → Par Card → Location.

Reporting a missing course

If you use Report a missing course, Par Card opens an email to support@forreallifestudio.com containing the course name, city and state, the app version, and, if you pinned the course, its map coordinates. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email from your own email app. We use it only to add the course, and we'll delete it on request.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

Par Card has no sign-up or login. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Par Card Pro is purchased in the app through the App Store. Payments are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Deleting your data

To delete your Par Card data, delete the Par Card app. If you use iCloud sync, also remove Par Card's iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or delete the app from every device. Rounds can be deleted individually from Records → History, and courses you added from the Add Course screen.

Children

Par Card is suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@forreallifestudio.com.
